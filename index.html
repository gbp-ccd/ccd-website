The Center for Collaborative Democracy is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to help every American reach their potential.

We bring together business leaders, consumer advocates, labor unions, environmentalists, civil rights groups and other major stakeholders to develop innovative solutions to some of our nation's most critical problems.

We see that process as necessary to reducing the hyper-polarization that threatens our democracy.
